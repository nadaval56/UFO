M/Sgt Lionel Hawkesworth, US Army Attache Office, informed the reporting
officer that he, with his wife and servant, observed a high vapor trail estimated
above 60,000 feet going east to west over Tehran. Source stated that this
observation was viewed for 34 minutes at 1154 hours local time on 23 Oct 57.

He stated that the object itself could not be seen, nor could any noise be heard.
The contrail was in a straight line in the east, where the vapor trail was first
formed and starting to disintegrate, it appeared that it was formed by an over
end rolling motion, The centrail entered or went above a high thin cirrus cloud
in the west and disappeared,

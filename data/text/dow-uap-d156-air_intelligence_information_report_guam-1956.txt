observed by an F-S6D pilot flying in the Guam radio range holding pattern on the

launched one hour and thirty-eight minutes (1:38) prior to the sighting. The balloon
wes lost from the view of the ground station one hour and ten minutes (1:10) prior
to the incident.

functioned and remained at a relatively low altitude, the Unidentified Flying Object
can not be readily explained,

GR INITIAL OBSERVATION. On 8 September 2021 a United States Special Operations
Forces (USSOF) AC-130 Gunship was conducting live fire training the Gulf of Oman in the
vicinity of (IVO) //MGRS: 40REN6&_4413{_4d/ during period of darkness, The AC-130 first
observed Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon (UAPs) when the aircraft dropped a
water-activated flare to use as a mock target. The AC-130 entered an orbit around the
flare and the Combat Systems Officer (CSO) conducted collateral scans with the aircraft's
Electro OpticalMnfrared (EO/IR) sensor IVO the flare. When the CSO slewed the sensor in
IR mode back to the flare they observed two approximately four-foot diameter cold orbs
between 0 to 20 feet above the surface of the water stationary over the flare for
approximately 15 seconds. After those 15 seconds passed, the CSO pulled the trigger
firing the aircraft's 105mm cannon. Immediately upon pulling the trigger, during the
slight delay between trigger pull and the cannon's recoil, the CSO observed the pair of
UAPs rapidly fly away from the flare and out of the sensor feed without changing

altitude,

MERE susSEQUENT OBSERVATIONS. The AC-130 crew observed approximately 25 instances of
UAPs viewed in IR as coki orbs in both white-is-hot an black-is-hot mode during the

sortie. Two UAPs were observed flying in a loose but coordinated formation increasing and
decreasing altitude like a wave while maneuvering around, over, and under each other in a
manner similar to how dolphins playfully swim in a pod. (SOURCE COMMENT: They [The UAPs]
knew they were flying together), UAPs were also separately observed flying in a formation

of three and separately observed flying erratically and maneuvering aggressively. The

attached videos depict a smoothly slewing EO/IR sensor or a stationary EO/IR sensor with
multiple UAPs darting around the sensor feed. The AC-130's crew attempted to save

entirety of the EO/IR footage of the UAP observations but the aircraft's Digital Video

Recorder (DVR) inexplicably failed and the video data became corrupted.

ESTIMATED SPEED. The CSO calculated the UAP’s approximate speeds using the
aircraft's TACTOOL and clocked their maximum observed maneuvering speed at 1300 miles per
hour (MPH) and the UAPs minimum observed maneuvering speed at 250 MPH. No means of
propulsion were observed,
